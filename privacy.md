This site is run by Milanda Wijekoon in Colombo, Sri Lanka. Questions? Email [{{ site.author.email }}](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}).

## What is collected

The site has no accounts, comments or sign-up forms. I do not sell your data. These services may collect data when you visit:

- **GitHub Pages (hosting)** logs your IP address and browser details.
- **Google Fonts** receives your IP address when the page loads its fonts.
- **Google Analytics** uses cookies to count visits and see which articles are read.
- **Google AdSense** may show ads and use cookies to personalise them.
- **Emails you send me** are used only to reply to you, and are deleted after about 24 months.

Your light/dark theme choice is saved in your own browser and never sent to me.

## Advertising and cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) or at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info). You can opt out of Google Analytics with the [opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Learn more about [how Google uses data from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

If you are in the EU, UK or Switzerland, personalised ads and non-essential cookies are used only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

## Where data goes

Google and GitHub process data in the United States and other countries, under their own privacy policies and legal safeguards for international transfers.

## Your rights

Under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, and the GDPR or CCPA where they apply, you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or withdraw consent. Email me and I will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka or your local regulator.

## Children

This site is not aimed at children under 16, and I do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date at the top.
