title: Leading a Team Without a Title Bump
category: Leadership
excerpt: >-
  What changed — and what didn't — when I started leading a 5-person product
  team while still writing code every day.
---

Leading a team while still shipping code every day means the two roles constantly compete for the same hours. What made it work wasn't doing both at full intensity — it was being deliberate about which one was in charge at any given moment.

## Protect deep work, but not by hiding

Blocking focus time only works if the team knows when you're reachable. A predictable rhythm of availability did more for trust than being technically online all day.

## Code review as the highest-leverage leadership tool

More than planning meetings, consistent, thoughtful code review turned out to be where architecture decisions actually got taught and reinforced — in context, on real code, in small doses.

## The part nobody warns you about

The hardest adjustment wasn't the extra responsibility — it was learning to let a teammate ship a solution that wasn't the one you would have written, when it was still a good one.
