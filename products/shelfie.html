Everything a home library needs

Scan to add

Point your camera at a book's barcode, or type the ISBN. Duplicates are caught: scanning a book you already own opens its existing entry.

Details filled in

Title, authors, publisher, description, categories and cover are looked up for you, and you can edit them before saving.

Add by hand

No barcode or online record? Enter the book manually. Only an ISBN and a title are required.

Cover photos

Photograph a book's cover and the app straightens and crops it for you.

Find things fast

Search by title, author, ISBN, publisher or category. Filter by category, language or read status, sort, and switch between grid and list.

Reading & lending

Mark books as read and see your totals. Note who you have lent a book to.

Install & first run

macOS

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Shelfie Home Library into Applications.
  2. The app is not yet signed by Apple. The first time, right-click it, choose Open, then confirm.
  3. If macOS says the app is “damaged and can't be opened”, it is not damaged. macOS flags downloaded unsigned apps. Click Cancel, open Terminal and run: 
    xattr -cr "/Applications/Shelfie Home Library.app"
    This only removes the quarantine flag from this one app and does not touch your books. Then open the app again.

Windows

  1. Run the downloaded .exe and follow the installer.
  2. If Windows SmartScreen warns that the app is unrecognised, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Using it

  1. Click Scan ISBN and allow camera access, or type the ISBN. Barcodes on the back cover start with 978 or 979.
  2. Check the details, edit if you like, then click Add to My Library.

Private by design

Shelfie does not collect any user data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your library is stored only on your own computer and is never uploaded anywhere.

  • Where your data lives: ~/Library/Application Support/HomeLibrary on macOS, %APPDATA%\HomeLibrary on Windows.
  • Network use: only book lookups by ISBN (Google Books, with Open Library as a fallback) and checks for new app versions. The app updates itself in the background and offers to restart.
  • Uninstalling does not delete your library. Remove the folder above if you want it gone.
